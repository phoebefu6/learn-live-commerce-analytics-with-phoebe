# Learn LIVE Commerce Analytics with Phoebe

Fourteen sessions on reading a TikTok Shop LIVE room as data. A two-hour stream is a video, a transcript, a comment feed and a per-minute sales ledger, recorded on four clocks. Line them up at the minute and every minute of the stream becomes a row: what the host said, what the camera showed, how many people were in the room, how many clicked the pinned product, how many bought. Then ask the question every MCN lead asks: what do the winning streams do that the rest do not. The first answer the data gives is wrong, and the course is about why.

**Live:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-live-commerce-analytics-with-phoebe/

Two tracks. **Leader, 6 sessions, no code:** read the room's numbers, unlearn the giveaway, design the say and the show, coach a replayed stream live, prove the playbook worked. **Builder, 8 sessions, Python:** the minute table, FFmpeg and the clock shift, detectors and the licence question, word-timestamped transcripts and a claims checker, per-minute scoring, pooled versus within-stream regression with LightGBM and SHAP, a five-minute nowcast, and the replay bench.

- `assets/live-engine.js` is **one generator with two homes**. Node runs it once to write `data/streams.csv` (60 streams, 7,200 minute rows, 26 columns) and the browser runs the same code for the replay bench, so every number on a page and every number in the bench come from the same seed. Re-running `node scripts/build-data.js` produces byte-identical files.
- **The causal structure is planted, on purpose, and said so.** Which segments raise clicks, which raise orders, how a giveaway opener buys platform traffic while lowering conversion all night, how an urgency pitch stops converting from its third consecutive minute, how an AI host loses the room a little faster each time the script repeats. The point is that the builder-track methods can be checked against a truth that is known. Nothing in it is a claim about TikTok's real ranking.
- **The finding:** nine of the ten best streams by GMV opened with a giveaway. A pooled regression prices the opener at plus 5,576 dollars. The same minutes demeaned by stream price it at minus 2.3 points of click-to-order rate. Both numbers are computed by `materials/analysis/analyze.py` and both are right; they answer different questions.
- **The bench ladder** (means over 40 seeded nights of tonight's stream): the host's own plan 18,198 dollars; cue a CTA on a dip plus 14.6 percent; product in hand on every CTA plus 9.7; answer the room every ten minutes plus 4.4; the playbook plus 27.3. The two cues a nervous room asks for: stretched urgency plus 0.5 percent with 3.2 policy-risk flags a night, and a mid-stream giveaway minus 11.2 percent.
- **TikTok Shop's own rules are on the pages, dated.** Metric names from the Seller Center academy (Views, Viewers, CTR, CTOR, Watch GPM), the four-stage diagnosis funnel, the misleading-content and fair-pricing wording, and the LIVE quality requirement effective 2026-09-17 that bans AI-generated voices and non-real-time narration on promotional LIVEs. A fully AI-voiced avatar host is a compliance stop on this platform; the dataset's AI-host streams show what the trade would be where one is allowed.
- Running artifact: a **minute table** and a **host playbook** for Lumen Glow, a fictional skincare brand with one 29 dollar serum, four human hosts and two AI avatars.
