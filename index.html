Learn LIVE Commerce Analytics with Phoebe

A two-hour TikTok Shop LIVE is a video, a transcript, a comment feed and a per-minute sales ledger, all recorded on slightly different clocks. Line them up and every minute of the stream becomes a row: what the host said, what the camera showed, how many people were in the room, how many clicked the pinned product, how many bought. This course does that to sixty streams of one skincare brand, four human hosts and two AI avatars, then asks the question every MCN lead asks: what do the winning streams do that the rest do not. The first answer the data gives is wrong, and the whole course is about why. Six leader sessions on judging a room and writing a playbook a host can follow; eight builder sessions that end with a replay bench where you cue the host mid-stream and the engine tells you what it cost or earned.

Leader track 🎯 · six sessions, no code

For whoever runs the hosts and answers for the GMV. How to read a LIVE room's numbers, why the obvious playbook is a traffic effect, and how to write one that a host can follow and a test can confirm.

📺 Leader session 1 · start here

Read the LIVE room

The funnel from views to orders, the numbers TikTok Shop reports per stream, and the split that runs the whole course: peak viewers tells you about the traffic a room was given, GPM tells you how well it sold what it was given.

▶ Start here 🏆 Leader session 2

What the top streams share

Nine of the ten best nights opened with a giveaway, and inside every stream the giveaway minutes convert worst. The traffic effect that makes "top streams do X" the most expensive sentence in the business.

 🗣️ Leader session 3

Design the say

Hook, product build, price anchor, CTA and urgency as a script with measured minute values. The two-minute urgency cue that converts at 15 percent and the third minute that drops it to 8.

 🎥 Leader session 4

Design the show

What the camera adds on top of the words: product in hand, the close-up, the price on screen. Where a human host beats an AI avatar per viewer and where the avatar's cheap daytime hours still pay.

 🎧 Leader session 5

Coach the room live

A five-minute nowcast in the producer's ear, the three cues worth giving and the two the room begs for when it gets nervous. Run the replay bench yourself and see which ones the engine pays for.

 📋 Leader session 6

Prove the playbook worked

The scorecard a host and a script can be judged on, the test design that separates a cue from the traffic it rode, and the urgency claims the platform and the regulator both read as deception.

Builder track 🔧 · eight sessions, one pipeline

Sixty streams of Lumen Glow, one hero SKU, turned into a minute-level table and analysed for real in Python. Session 8 runs the finished playbook on tonight's stream and measures it over forty seeded nights.

🧱 Builder session 1 · start here

The stream as data

Four sources, one grain: the video, the transcript, the comment feed and the per-minute ledger become one row per minute. The event model and the metric definitions everything downstream inherits.

▶ Start here ⏱️ Builder session 2

Ingest and align

FFmpeg segments, frames at 1 fps, 16 kHz audio, and the clock problem: a stream reaches viewers seconds after the host says it, so the sale lands in the wrong minute unless you shift it.

 👁️ Builder session 3

See it: what the camera shows

Product in hand, close-up ratio, host on camera, scene cuts and the price card read by OCR. Open-vocabulary detection against a named SKU, and the licence question that decides which detector you can ship.

 🎙️ Builder session 4

Hear it: what the host says

Word-timestamped transcripts, a segment tagger for hook, build, demo, price, CTA, urgency and Q&A, and a claims checker that flags the phrases a platform policy reads as deceptive.

 📊 Builder session 5

Score the content

Per-minute click rate, order rate and orders per thousand viewer-minutes by segment. The table where CTA minutes order at 11.6 per thousand and a hook orders at 2.0, and why a ratio on a small denominator lies.

 🧭 Builder session 6

From traits to a playbook

The pooled regression that prices a giveaway opener at plus 5,576 dollars, the within-stream version that prices the same minutes at minus 2.3 points of conversion, and a LightGBM with SHAP that agrees with the second.

 📡 Builder session 7

The live nowcast

Forecast the next five minutes of orders from the room so far. A smoothed order rate times a viewer projection, backtested over 6,720 windows, and the alert rule that does not fire on noise.

 🎛️ Builder session 8

Intervene, then prove it

The replay bench: cue the host at any minute of tonight's stream, run the same plan over forty nights, and read seven policies against the host's own. Then the switchback design that makes the result believable.

What is real here 🧾

A course about separating a real effect from a traffic effect should be exact about which of its own numbers are measured, and which were planted so the methods could be checked against a known truth.
